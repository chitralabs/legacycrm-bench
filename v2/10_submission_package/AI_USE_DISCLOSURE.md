# AI-Use Disclosure (accurate; to be reviewed and approved by the human authors)

Per IEEE submission policy (AI-generated content shall be disclosed in the Acknowledgments
section; grammar/editing-only use is exempt — see 00_project_admin/OJCS_CURRENT_REQUIREMENTS.md
for the sourced policy text), this project's AI use is **substantially more than grammar
editing** and therefore MUST be disclosed. The following statement reflects what actually
happened in this repository and must be kept accurate if work continues:

## Disclosure statement (for the Acknowledgments section)

"Generative AI (Claude, Anthropic) was used, under the direction and review of the human
authors, to assist in: drafting manuscript text; designing and implementing the benchmark
artifacts (the synthetic Meridian CRM 4.2 legacy specification, benchmark cases, acceptance
tests, and reference solutions); implementing the evaluation harness and analysis scripts; and
compiling the literature search. All benchmark semantics, ground-truth derivations, and
verification results were validated by executable tests; all references were verified against
primary sources; and the human authors reviewed and take full responsibility for all content."
